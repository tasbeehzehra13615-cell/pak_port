NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to read its answer.

What is PakPort?

PakPort is a personal shopping service concept designed to help overseas Pakistanis request products from Pakistani markets and stores.

What products can I request?

You can submit requests for suitable products from local markets, boutiques and Pakistani brands. Availability depends on the item and seller.

How do I request a product?

Click any Request a Product button and fill out our Google Form with the product name, link or description.

How much does the service cost?

The cost would depend on the product, service fee and shipping destination. A real service should confirm estimated costs before purchase.

Can PakPort deliver to my country?

International delivery would depend on the destination, shipping availability, customs rules and the product being shipped.

Does submitting the form place an order?

No. The form is a request for information and does not itself confirm a purchase or payment.
